How was this computed?

Estimates the FAA’s nationwide lifting announcement.

01

Prices → CDF

Each YES contract pays $1 if the announcement precedes its deadline. We treat its bid/ask midpoint as an approximate probability.

p = (bid + ask) / 2

We fit a nondecreasing curve, giving tighter spreads more weight. Known NO/YES settlements are fixed at 0%/100%.

02

ETA and range

The ETA is the CDF’s 50% crossing, interpolated between deadlines. The middle 50% range spans the 25th–75th percentiles. Unbracketed endpoints stay “before” or “after.”

The bid/ask band shows price sensitivity, not a statistical confidence interval.

03

Density

density = ΔCDF / Δhours

Area gives interval probability. Negative raw slopes are invalid densities. Unobserved tails are not filled in or normalized away.

04

Historical snapshots

Each minute uses its closing quotes and settlements known then. Stale or invalid quotes are excluded. The dashed line marks observations after reopening; the calculation stays the same.

The green point is the reported 8:15 p.m. ET reopening. Kalshi’s outcomes place the qualifying announcement from 8:00 p.m. to before 8:30 p.m.. The reopening time does not enter the model.

Fit details and mean estimate

Weighted isotonic regression minimizes:

Σ wi(Fi − pi)²,   Fi ≤ Fi+1
wi = 1 / max(aski − bidi, 0.02)²

Missing, crossed, stale or inactive quotes and spreads ≥98 percentage points are excluded.

The mean requires a first supported point A at 0%. Beyond the last deadline L, it uses the additional wait selected below.

E[T] ≈ A + ∫AL [1 − F(t)] dt + [1 − F(L)] × tail wait
